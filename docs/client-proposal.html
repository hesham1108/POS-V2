Product plan & design system · v1.0

A fast, offline cashier and store system, built for one shop floor.

This document brings together the original UI mockups, the technical spec, and the detailed functional blueprint into one plan. It covers what the system does, how cashiers and managers move through it, how it looks, and the order we will deliver it in.

01 · Overview

What we are building

StoresPOS runs on a computer inside the store. Cashiers sell from a single fast screen; owners and managers handle stock, purchases, suppliers, customers, shifts and reports from the same app. Nothing depends on the internet.

Runs onStore PC, local network
DatabaseSQL Server (local)
UsersAdmin · Manager · Cashier
CurrencyEGP, 2 decimals

Faster checkout

Barcode or search, one-key shortcuts (F2, F4, F8, F9), and payment plus printing in a single step.

Drawer you can trust

Every sale, refund and cash-out lands on the open shift. Expected cash is always visible, and cash is kept separate from card and wallet payments.

Accurate stock and prices

Purchases raise stock and refresh selling prices from your markup. Old invoices keep the prices they were sold at.

Technology

ASP.NET Core MVC with Razor views, a three-layer structure (screens → business rules → data), Entity Framework Core on SQL Server, and secure sign-in with hashed passwords and role-based access. All fonts, scripts and styles ship with the installation, so the app never calls out to the web.

02 · Confirmed rules

Business rules agreed so far

These came from the mockups and our discussions. The design and the code both follow them; changing any of them is a scope change.

One open shift, many cashiers

The store has one open shift at a time. A second cashier who logs in joins it as a participant. Each cashier's sales and cash-outs are still tracked individually.

Shifts never close on their own

Logging out does not close a shift, and an old shift is never closed because of its date. Closing is a deliberate action by a permitted user.

Opening amount is optional

Leave it blank and it counts as 0. The cashier just confirms and starts selling.

Counting cash at close is optional

If an actual amount is entered, the system shows surplus, shortage or matched. If it is left blank, the shift is marked Not Reconciled, never "0 counted".

Only cash counts toward the drawer

Visa/Card, InstaPay and Vodafone Cash appear in sales and reports but are not part of expected drawer cash.

Price edits stay on the invoice

Changing a price at the till affects that invoice line only. The catalog price stays the same. Both prices and the user who changed it are saved.

Cash-out = amount + reason

No separate vault. A cash-out is linked to the open shift and the user, and lowers expected drawer cash.

Markup drives selling prices

Enter a price and the markup is calculated, or the other way round. A new purchase cost recalculates retail and wholesale prices from their stored markups.

06 · Design system

Look and feel

Modern, premium and sleek, built on the mockups' deep green. Quiet surfaces with fine hairline borders, soft layered depth, a confident geometric display face for headings and totals, and a dark mode for evening shifts. The polish comes from precise spacing and type rather than decoration, so the till stays fast and calm all day.

Sleek

Hairline 1 px borders, generous whitespace, no heavy boxes or clutter.

Premium

Layered soft shadows, 16–24 px rounded corners, subtle gradient on primary actions.

Modern

Manrope display type, light and dark themes, smooth 150–200 ms transitions.

Still practical

High contrast for money and status. Effects never slow the till down.

Speed first

The keyboard and scanner can do everything at the till. The mouse is optional.

Money is the loudest thing

Totals and prices get the biggest, boldest type on every screen.

One accent

Green means "do this" or "all good". Everything else stays neutral.

Same app, both directions

Every screen is built to mirror cleanly between Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR).

13 · Decisions needed

What we need from you

These choices change how parts of the system behave. Each has our suggestion; we only need a yes or an alternative.

Cash drawersOne drawer for the whole store, or one per till?
Suggested: one shared drawer, matching the one-open-shift model.
Split paymentsCan one invoice be paid partly in cash and partly by card?
Suggested: not in version 1; add later if needed.
Held invoicesHow long do held invoices stay, and do they survive closing the shift?
Suggested: kept until the shift closes, then listed for cancel or resume.
ReturnsWho can approve a return, and is there a time limit?
Suggested: manager approval, within 14 days.
Selling below zero stockAllow a sale when the system shows no stock left?
Suggested: block it, with a manager override.
TaxAre prices shown with VAT included, and at what rate?
Suggested: prices include 14% VAT, shown on the receipt.
Profit calculationWhich cost is used for profit reports?
Suggested: weighted average cost.
BackupsHow often, and where to?
Suggested: automatic daily plus one at each shift close, to a second drive or USB.
LicensingHow is the installation activated offline?
Suggested: license file tied to the PC, issued by us.
DigitsArabic-Indic (١٢٣) or Western (123) digits on screen and receipts?
Suggested: Western on screen, choice for receipts.
